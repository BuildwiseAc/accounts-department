We use our dedicated systems, app and workflows to support your day-to-day accounts — so invoices, expenses and submissions are managed in an organised way, leaving you free to focus on the job.
What we take care of
Sales Invoices
We help raise invoices with CIS deductions, retention and VAT domestic reverse charge considered — so your billing reflects the right treatment for your work.
Purchase Invoices & Expenses
Costs coming into your business — materials, equipment, site costs — are logged and tracked based on what you provide, with payment due dates noted and records kept in order.
Labour & Payments
We support the labour payment process — verifying workers with HMRC, applying the appropriate CIS deduction rate, processing payments and issuing monthly CIS payslips.
CIS Returns
Monthly CIS returns submitted to HMRC — covering workers paid, deductions made and statements issued. Whether you're a contractor, subcontractor or both, we handle the filing.
MTD Submissions
Making Tax Digital requires quarterly digital submissions to HMRC from April 2026. We prepare and submit returns on time, keeping your records in a compliant digital format.
Self Assessment
Your annual tax return is prepared based on the information provided, with allowances considered and the return submitted ahead of the deadline.
VAT Returns
VAT returns filed on time with domestic reverse charge rules for construction applied. Where applicable, VAT on business expenses is also considered as part of the return.
CIS Suffered & Refunds
CIS deductions from your payments are tracked throughout the year. Where a refund position arises, we work to reclaim what you're due from HMRC on your behalf.
